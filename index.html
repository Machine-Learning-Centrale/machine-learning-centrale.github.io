Course Description

This course is an introduction to machine learning, with its methods and its mathematical foundations. We study supervised learning: empirical risk minimization, the bias-variance trade-off, classification and logistic regression. We then study decision trees and ensemble methods: random forests, bagging and boosting. The course continues with deep learning (neural networks, backpropagation, stochastic gradient descent) and with unsupervised learning (dimensionality reduction). Lectures alternate with exercise sessions and hands-on sessions in Python. Students also work in groups on a project with a real dataset.

"Nothing is more practical than a good theory." V. Vapnik

Evaluation

  • Projects. Students work in groups on a project with a real dataset. The defences take place during the last session, on Wednesday 31 March 2027 (4 hours). The project counts for 50% of the final grade.
  • Final exam. A multiple-choice quiz. Some questions have one correct answer, others have zero, one or several correct answers. Wrong answers may give negative points. No computer and no calculator. Handwritten notes are allowed. The quiz counts for 50% of the final grade.
